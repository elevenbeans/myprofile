雅加达 Jakarta

印度尼西亚 · 6.1754°S · 106.8272°E · Asia/Jakarta

04About Jakarta

Jakarta is a city in Indonesia at 6.1754°S · 106.8272°E (Asia/Jakarta). Earth Online frames Monas · Merdeka Square with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Monas turns Merdeka Square into Jakarta’s axis with a 137 m obelisk and flame.
