罗马 Rome

04About Rome

Rome is a city in Italy at 41.9028°N · 12.4964°E (Europe/Rome). Earth Online frames Colosseum · Roman Forum with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. The Colosseum keeps ancient Rome’s public scale in a 48 m elliptical façade.
