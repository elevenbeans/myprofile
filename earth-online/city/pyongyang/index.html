04About Pyongyang

Pyongyang is a city in North Korea at 39.0392°N · 125.7625°E (Asia/Pyongyang). Earth Online frames Ryugyong Hotel · Taedong with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Ryugyong Hotel’s 330 m, 105-storey pyramid remains a landmark precisely because it is unfinished.
