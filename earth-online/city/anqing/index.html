04About Anqing

Anqing is a city in China at 30.5036°N · 117.0493°E (Asia/Shanghai). Earth Online frames Zhenfeng Pagoda · Yingjiang Temple with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Zhenfeng Pagoda rises between Yingjiang Temple and the Yangtze, tying Anqing’s old town to the river in one vertical axis.
