04About Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur is a city in Malaysia at 3.1579°N · 101.7117°E (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur). Earth Online frames Petronas Towers · KLCC with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. The Petronas Towers pair 452 m shafts with a skybridge at the centre of Kuala Lumpur.
