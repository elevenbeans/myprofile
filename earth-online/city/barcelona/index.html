04About Barcelona

Barcelona is a city in Spain at 41.4036°N · 2.1744°E (Europe/Madrid). Earth Online frames Sagrada Família · Eixample with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Sagrada Família’s unfinished 172 m towers turn Barcelona’s grid into a model that keeps growing.
