04About Qianxian

Qianxian is a city in China at 34.5755°N · 108.2139°E (Asia/Shanghai). Earth Online frames Qianling Mausoleum · Liangshan with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Qianling folds the whole Liangshan ridge into a tomb landscape, with the spirit way and stone guardians following the terrain rather than a tower.
