04About Nantong

Nantong is a city in China at 31.9802°N · 120.8943°E (Asia/Shanghai). Earth Online frames Hao River · Nantong Old Town with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. The Hao River loops Nantong’s old town into a closed water axis, with low lanes and embankments forming its visible edge.
