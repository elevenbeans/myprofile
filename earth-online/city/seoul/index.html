首尔 Seoul

韩国 · 37.5665°N · 126.9780°E · Asia/Seoul

04About Seoul

Seoul is a city in South Korea at 37.5665°N · 126.9780°E (Asia/Seoul). Earth Online frames N Seoul Tower · Namsan with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. N Seoul Tower uses Namsan’s ridge as much as its own height to mark the city.
