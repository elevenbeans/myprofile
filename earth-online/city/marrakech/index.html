04About Marrakech

Marrakech is a city in Morocco at 31.6250°N · 7.9900°W (Africa/Casablanca). Earth Online frames Jemaa el-Fna · Koutoubia with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. Jemaa el-Fna is a low medina ensemble of minaret, market and square rather than a tower.
