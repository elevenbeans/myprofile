开罗 Cairo

04About Cairo

Cairo is a city in Egypt at 30.0444°N · 31.2357°E (Africa/Cairo). Earth Online frames Giza Pyramids · Nile with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. The Giza pyramids hold a 139 m triangle against the plateau, still legible beyond Cairo.
