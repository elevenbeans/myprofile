柏林 Berlin

04About Berlin

Berlin is a city in Germany at 52.5200°N · 13.4050°E (Europe/Berlin). Earth Online frames Brandenburg Gate · Unter den Linden with OpenStreetMap massing and a 3D landmark. The Brandenburg Gate’s 26 m colonnade turns Unter den Linden into a ceremonial axis.
